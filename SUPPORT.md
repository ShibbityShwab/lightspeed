## 🐛 Bug Reports

Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ShibbityShwab/lightspeed/issues/new?template=bug_report.md).

Include:
- Your OS and LightSpeed version
- The game you were playing
- Log output (`RUST_LOG=debug lightspeed ...`)
