# Licensing and ownership

> This page summarises how LightSpeed is licensed and how contributions are
> handled. The controlling documents are [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) and
> [`CLA.md`](../CLA.md); if they disagree with this page, they win.

## Who owns LightSpeed

LightSpeed is a proprietary work owned solely by
[ShibbityShwab](https://github.com/ShibbityShwab). The Owner is the only party
with authority to license, relicense, distribute, or commercially exploit the
Software. Using the Software or contributing to it does not make anyone a
copyright holder, co-owner, or licensor.

## What users may do

[`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) (LightSpeed Software License v1.1) grants free use for
Non-Commercial purposes - personal gaming, education, research, open-source
contribution to this project, and internal evaluation. Commercial use requires
a separate commercial license from the Owner.

Third-party data and components keep their own licenses; see
[`NOTICE`](../NOTICE).

## How contributions work

Contributions are accepted only under [`CLA.md`](../CLA.md). The CLA assigns
the copyright in a contribution to the Owner, so the Project keeps a single
owner and can be licensed - including commercially - without tracking down
every past contributor.

How to accept:

1. Open a pull request.
2. Check the Contributor License Agreement box in the pull-request template
   (or post the acceptance statement from `CLA.md` section 10).
3. The Owner will not merge a contribution without acceptance.

The CLA also requires that contributors are natural persons and that AI tools
or agents are never listed as authors or co-authors. Commits name only the
human who wrote them; tool names, session links, and
`Co-authored-by`/`Co-Authored-By` trailers for AI tools or agents are not
accepted.

## Contributions made before the CLA

Contributions merged before 2026-11-17 remain under the license terms in force
when they were submitted, unless the contributor signs the CLA. The Owner may
ask a past contributor to sign; until then, those contributions are not
covered by the assignment in `CLA.md`.

## Questions

For commercial licensing, contribution questions, or a CLA signature, open a
GitHub issue titled "[Commercial License]" or "[CLA]", or contact
[@ShibbityShwab](https://github.com/ShibbityShwab).
